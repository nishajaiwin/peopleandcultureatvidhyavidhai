Policy Library

Summarised from the Team Handbook. The handbook is a living document — Vidhya Vidhai reserves the right to revise or discontinue any term in it, so treat the handbook itself as the authority where the two differ.

Safeguarding and statutory duties

Non-negotiable

Child Safeguard Policy

The duty to ensure our staff, operations and programmes do no harm to children.

  • Covers sexual abuse, physical abuse, exploitation, emotional abuse and neglect
  • Child Protection Committee: Chairperson and Executive Director, a Child Protection Officer, a senior staff member and an external NGO member
  • The committee handles only child-safety concerns, records them, recommends action to the co-founding team and closes each case within a set time
  • It convenes as soon as an incident is reported and cooperates with police, judiciary and local administration while protecting the child's privacy
  • Reporting: alter or avoid children's names, blur faces, and get consent from the child and their parent or guardian before anything goes online
  • Never meet a child in isolation — interactions happen in groups or with a guardian, parent or teacher present

Applies to board and management, permanent and contract team members, volunteers, consultants, outsourced workers, vendors, visitors, partners and collaborators

POSH Policy

Prohibition, prevention and redressal of sexual harassment, under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act.

  • Covers physical contact and advances, demands for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, displaying pornography, and any other unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature
  • Also covers preferential or detrimental treatment tied to such conduct, a hostile work environment, humiliating treatment, and negative attention based on sexual orientation
  • Internal Complaints Committee: Karunya, Gomathi Shankar and Regila — raise a complaint with any or all of them
  • Complaints must be raised within three months of the incident, or of the last incident in a series
  • First offence: interview with the CEO and a written warning on file. Second: a signed letter from employee and manager, plus a second warning. Beyond that: termination
  • Warnings stay on record for one year. Good-faith complaints that cannot be substantiated are never treated as false; retaliation is prohibited

Applies to everyone at Vidhya Vidhai

Anti-Discrimination and Anti-Harassment

A workplace that is inclusive, respectful and safe for everyone.

  • No verbal, physical, visual or written harassment — including offensive jokes, slurs and derogatory comments
  • No conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive environment
  • No discrimination on race, colour, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity or any other legally protected status

Applies to all team members and applicants

Conduct and culture

How we treat each other

General Terms of Employment

The commitments each team member makes on joining — written in the first person, because they are promises rather than rules.

  • Take ownership of your own actions and behaviour towards stakeholders, team and partners
  • Do not discuss or encourage conversation about salaries, hikes or increments, or compare your salary to a colleague's
  • Keep confidences; raise anything that could affect the organisation with the founders
  • Bring stakeholders, partners and funders into the loop with founders or managers — cc them, or share minutes beforehand
  • Do not form informal WhatsApp or other groups without the organisation's consent
  • Reach out to leadership at any point of struggle or disagreement, without waiting for a review
  • No part-time or consulting engagements; align with leadership before committing to higher studies

Applies to all team members

Non-Judgemental Space and Confidentiality

Vidhya Vidhai is built by people from diverse backgrounds and beliefs. Protecting that is everyone's job.

  • Do not press personal beliefs in a way that affects the culture, and do not make jokes about others' beliefs or life choices
  • Labelling people by their personal choices leads to unfair judgement, stereotyping and discrimination
  • Sensitive information shared in reflections, team meetings or by stakeholders stays confidential
  • Anything that affects the organisation or a programme must be shared with the co-founding or leadership team

Applies to all team members

Communication Policy

Keeping the team informed about when you can be reached.

  • Communicate your intended start and end times for the day
  • Your presence or absence should not put undue stress on colleagues or their deliverables
  • Raise exceptional circumstances early rather than letting them surface late

Applies to all team members

Working norms

Day to day

Attendance Policy

Accountability and trust rather than rigid hours.

  • Check in and out each day on the Zoho People app
  • Flexible hours, judged on deliverables — structure your day around your responsibilities
  • Check out for personal breaks and check back in when you resume
  • Checking in while travelling is not permitted; attendance should reflect actual hours at your workspace
  • Miss check-in or check-out on more than three days in a month and every further instance counts as leave

Applies to all team members

Work From Home Policy

Twelve WFH days a year, plus every Saturday.

  • 12 days a year, maximum 6 in any six-month period; carried forward within the year but not into the next
  • 1–2 days: no approval, inform your reporting head and the team in advance
  • 3–6 days: three weeks' notice, approved by the Programme Heads and the P&C team
  • 7 or more days: two months' notice, approved by the Co-Founding Team
  • On any WFH day other than Saturday, send a planner email by 9:30 am and a completion update by 7:00 pm
  • All Saturdays are WFH. Update pending and next week's goals by 4 pm; team leads check at 9 am Monday, and a missed update is marked as leave
  • If the programme needs you in the field or the office, the programme takes priority

Applies to all team members

Leave Policy

Time off for rest, recuperation and social obligations. Full entitlements sit under Rewards & Benefits.

  • Leave year runs 1 April to 31 March, credited at the start of the year
  • Applies to permanent employees and contract workers with at least three months' service; leave is pro-rated for those who join mid-year
  • 1–2 days is auto-approved; 3–6 days needs three weeks' notice and Programme Head plus HR approval; 7 or more needs two months' notice and the Co-Founding Team
  • Apply on Zoho before 5 pm on the day you return, or the leave becomes loss of pay
  • More than five short-notice leaves triggers a conversation with your team lead
  • Leave taken without approval is absence from duty — unpaid, with a written explanation and a return-to-work interview with the CEO
  • Volunteers and interns follow a separate leave policy

Applies to full-time and part-time staff, contract staff and external consultants

Travel and Reimbursement Policy

What the organisation covers when you travel for work. Rates are listed under Rewards & Benefits.

  • Plan journeys to avoid arriving between 10:30 pm and 5:00 am
  • Plan lodging directly with the finance team; organisation-partnered options come first
  • Vouchers must be signed by your manager before being uploaded to Zoho Expense
  • Home to district office or collectorate, and back, is not reimbursable

Applies to all team members who travel for work

Bike Rental Policy

Rented bikes for reaching remote schools — DEEP programmes only.

  • A valid driver's licence and a vehicle orientation before the first rental
  • Official school visits only; personal use is prohibited
  • Pre- and post-rental inspections are mandatory
  • Always wear a helmet; no reckless driving, speeding or phone use
  • Report issues, accidents or damage promptly with photos, cc'ing the Manager and Programme Director
  • Damage caused by negligence is deducted from reimbursements or salary; serious safety breaches mean immediate suspension

Applies to DEEP programme staff and authorised personnel

Procurement Policy

Transparency, value for money, accountability, compliance and ethics in every purchase.

  • Three vendor quotations for every procurement, with price, GST and HSN or SAC codes
  • Purchases below ₹10,000 may not need managerial approval; ₹10,001 and above need the Programme Director
  • Issue a formal Purchase Order, then a Goods Received Note on delivery
  • Pay only after receipt, a GST-compliant invoice and code verification
  • Keep all procurement records for at least five years; the policy is reviewed annually
  • Breaches lead to disciplinary action, and severe ones to legal proceedings

Applies to all procurement, whatever the funding source

Joining and leaving

Both ends of the journey

Joining, Induction and Probation

A structured one-month plan, week by week, with a buddy alongside.

  • Orientation covers mission, vision and values, your department and team structure, the policies, and the tools for your role
  • A 30-day induction plan — "Learn & Do and Do & Learn" — plus introductions to key team members and stakeholders
  • Probation is the first three months, and may lengthen or shorten based on performance
  • Expectations are set at the start of probation and assessed at the end, with regular feedback in between

Applies to all new team members

Relocation Policy

Support when the organisation asks you to move. Limits are under Rewards & Benefits.

  • Prior written approval from your Reporting Manager and HR before any expense
  • File relocation expenses separately from other reimbursements and mark them clearly
  • Get three estimates from national residential movers before accepting a proposal
  • Submit air travel agendas 15 days in advance for approval
  • Benefits do not apply if a guest house is arranged, or if you stay with a friend or relative
  • A No Objection Certificate signed by stakeholders, IT and HR is required

Applies to employees relocating at the organisation's request

Happy Exit Policy

Every journey is valuable, including its end. Settlement figures are under Rewards & Benefits.

  • Give at least eight weeks' notice by email to the co-founding team and your team lead
  • Short notice: you are encouraged to donate one month's salary as a goodwill gesture towards the transition
  • Send a formal email to the whole team once your exit plan is agreed
  • An exit interview with HR covers your experience, feedback on policies and culture, and suggestions for onboarding and exit
  • Hand over pending tasks and documentation before your last day, and submit all credentials to HR
  • Alumni network access, plus openness to future collaboration or rejoining

Applies to every departing team member

25 to 31 December is organisation downtime, every year.

  • No official work runs in that week — time for travel, family and well-being
  • Tell external partners and stakeholders at least a month in advance

Probation

  • May lengthen or shorten based on performance, followed by a review conversation
